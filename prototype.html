How levels are determined

LevelEvidence required
L0 ParticipationCourse completed. No assessment, or the assessment did not meet integrity criteria.
L1 AssessedAuto-graded quiz passed, at least the minimum time spent, and at most 3 attempts per 72 hours. Also the cap for exams with no identity record.
L2 VerifiedProctored or project-based exam passed, with an identity check on record.

Levels only move up on evidence. Engagement signals can add a flag, never raise a level. Corporate enrollment is shown as context and does not change the level. Older certificates need the learner's last initial and issue year. Certificates issued before the cutover date are labelled Legacy and are levelled from the records we hold; if timing or attempt records are missing, they stay at L0. A reported concern is checked by our team first; the level is hidden only if the concern is accepted for review.
