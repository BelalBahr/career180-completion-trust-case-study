# Demo video narration script

Record your own voice over `demo-video-silent.mp4` (about 2 minutes 26 seconds). The time is when each part starts on screen.

**0:00**  This is a short walkthrough of the certificate check prototype for the Completion Trust problem.

**0:06**  A partner enters a certificate ID and sees the outcome, together with the evidence behind it. The sample data covers courses with no assessment, quizzes, and proctored or project exams, for both corporate and self-directed learners.

**0:22**  First, a course with no assessment. The page says Level 0, Participation. The learner completed the course, but competence was not assessed. We never imply more than the evidence supports.

**0:36**  Second, an auto-graded quiz. The learner passed, spent enough time, and used one attempt, so this is Level 1, Assessed.

**0:44**  Third, a corporate learner who passed the quiz too fast. The level drops to Level 0 with an integrity flag. Being a corporate learner never changes the level. Only the evidence does.

**0:57**  Fourth, a proctored exam with an identity check on record. This is Level 2, Verified.

**1:04**  Fifth, a project exam with no identity record. It is capped at Level 1, because Verified requires confirmed identity.

**1:13**  Sixth, a course that was revised after this certificate was issued. The level stays the same, but the partner sees that the course has changed since, from version 2 to version 4.

**1:25**  Seventh, older certificates. They first ask for the learner's last initial and issue year, to protect privacy. Then the page shows the Legacy label. The assessment history is missing, so the level stays at zero.

**1:39**  Eighth, reporting a concern. The partner gives a reason and an email. The certificate stays visible with a banner while our team checks it. Nothing is hidden until ops accepts the concern.

**1:52**  Once a concern is accepted, the certificate is Under review, and the level and evidence are hidden.

**1:58**  After review, a certificate can be revoked, with the reason shown.

**2:04**  An unknown ID gets a generic message, so IDs cannot be probed.

**2:10**  Every level has documented, checkable criteria. This is the methodology that Legal signs off on.

**2:18**  Evidence, not a completion flag. Completion rules stay the same, and partners see what was actually demonstrated.
